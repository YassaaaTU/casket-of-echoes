# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Personal Casket with auto-sorted category tabs, search, sorting, large stacks and an inspect pane
- Rest Points (lit campfires, the Bottomless Casket and datapack-defined blocks), Rest & Replenish, pickup overflow
- Build Echoes, Keepsakes, Reliquary Shards, Echo Pouches, Vestige Moth and Spectral Casket
- Casket Attunement, remote-access cost, combat lock, optional Equip Load
- Parties: built-in, FTB Teams, Open Parties and Claims and vanilla teams; Covenant Casket; party HUD with 3D portraits
- Teammate markers in the world and on Xaero's Minimap and World Map
- Integrations: Curios, Epic Fight, JEI, EMI, REI, FTB Quests, Quark, HungerStrike
